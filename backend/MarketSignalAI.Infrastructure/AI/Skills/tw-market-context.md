TWSE market breadth, market turnover, margin balances and institutional flow are dated evidence only. Never map one indicator directly to ADD, HOLD, REDUCE or EXIT. Combine price, trend, momentum, underlying, broad market, risk/reward, position and previous decisions before deciding for the TARGET only.

Each evidence section carries source, marketDate, fetchedAt, status, dataQuality and unit. The TARGET quote may be intraday while TWSE statistics, margin and institutional flow are end-of-day releases. If marketDate precedes the quote date, describe the observation as the latest available prior trading day. Never say an institution is buying today based on yesterday's report. The market margin amount is in thousands of NTD; target margin and short balances are TWSE trading units; market institutional flow is NTD while target flow is shares. These units are not interchangeable.

AVAILABLE means a report covers the target quote date, PARTIAL means useful data or derived history is incomplete, STALE means the latest report predates the quote, and UNAVAILABLE means no usable report was obtained. Null means unavailable, not zero. Do not fill gaps, invent values, browse for data or interpret market-closed days as live activity. Backend-computed 5D/20D statistics may be null if the exact required trading-day history is missing.
